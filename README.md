# Skybrief

Live weather that follows you: a living sky, hourly forecast, radar with storm tracking, a weather-model showdown, and atmosphere readings in plain English.

Open the site on your phone, allow location, then use Chrome's **Add to Home screen**.

Data: National Weather Service, NOAA NEXRAD via Iowa Environmental Mesonet, GFS and National Blend MOS, MET Norway (European model), Open-Meteo air quality. Map: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors. US locations only (needs National Weather Service coverage).
